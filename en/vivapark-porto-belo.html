What’s already open at Vivapark

While many planned neighborhoods are still selling scale models, life is already happening here. This is what you’ll find today:

  1. Lakeside boulevardOpen

    Restaurants and cafés facing the water. It’s where the neighborhood meets at the end of the day.

  2. Tennis, padel and beach tennis courtsOpen

    Open since day one, next to beach volleyball, MUV Arena and Wave gym.

  3. Official Paris Saint-Germain academyOpen

    PSG Academy brought the French club’s football training into the neighborhood, for boys and girls.

  4. Colégio Bom JesusOpen

    Open since February 2025, from preschool to high school, with room for 1,000 students.

  5. A college on the wayOn the way

    A Uniavan campus is being planned inside the neighborhood, bringing higher education close to home.

  6. Own sewage treatmentOpen

    The neighborhood’s own treatment plant: wastewater is treated before returning to nature.

  7. 24-hour monitoringOpen

    Cameras with facial and license-plate recognition, virtual fences and access control.

The neighborhood

A place designed for living, not just for housing

Vivapark was conceived by urban planner Jaime Lerner and is developed by Vokkan. It opened in October 2023 at the entrance of Porto Belo, next to Jardim Dourado, and the developer presents it as Brazil’s first park neighborhood.

The idea is simple: tree-lined streets, parks, lakes and shops in one place, without walls with security powered by technology. The developer expects up to 35,000 residents once the neighborhood is complete.

Vision Home Club

The neighborhood outside. The club at home.

Vision is a partnership between Vokkan and Grupo N1, in the heart of Vivapark: three residential towers with shops on the ground floor and amenities designed like a club, for everyday use without crossing town.
